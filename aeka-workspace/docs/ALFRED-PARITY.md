# alfred_ and Aeka Workspace: Feature Comparison

Aeka Advisors India LLP · 27 October 2026

alfred_ (get-alfred.ai) is an "AI executive assistant" for Gmail or Outlook, calendar and tasks,
aimed at founders and consultants. Its features were reconstructed from its own help centre, product
and blog pages and from two independent reviews (sources at the end). Most of its claims are
self-reported; no authenticated walkthrough was possible. Aeka Workspace now covers every capability
found, except calendar writes, which the firm chose to keep read only.

## Inbox

| alfred_                                                                                                             | Aeka Workspace                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Overnight triage: urgency score and action type (needs reply, needs decision, FYI, noise)                           | Four tiers with written rules, eleven categories and six handling values; full email and thread, Indian tax context; built-in rules and the member's rules decide without AI where they can; each answer validated |
| Sender importance (VIP, titles, follow-up count, age)                                                               | VIP list by person or domain (at least Important, never Urgent on status alone, never archived); "Chased N times" count given to the AI and shown                                                                  |
| Noise archived (off by default)                                                                                     | Archive one, many (confirmed) or, if switched on, low-priority bulk mail automatically; never VIPs or mail linked to a mandate; counted only when Outlook confirms                                                 |
| Drafts prepared for messages that need a reply                                                                      | "Draft a reply?" asked only where a reply is expected; drafted on request only, to save AI cost; missing facts marked `[INPUT REQUIRED: ...]`                                                                      |
| Approve before sending: send, edit or skip                                                                          | Send only after review, never with a placeholder left; unconfirmed sends never shown as sent                                                                                                                       |
| Voice learned from sent mail; per-recipient profiles; learns from edits; exclude contacts                           | Writing style learned from 40 sent emails and accepted by the member before use; greeting and sign-off last used with each person; the last three material edits used as examples; "Never send to AI" rules        |
| Remembers who you are waiting on and who is waiting on you; escalates first to third follow-up; flags 48-hour waits | Follow-ups page: waiting on others (levels at 2, 5 and 10 days) and people chasing you; snooze, done, reopen; follow-up drafts on request; in the brief, Today, chat and alerts                                    |
| Commitments ("I'll send this by Friday") surfaced                                                                   | Promises in sent mail found (word check first, then one AI call per batch), each a to-do with its date, linked to the email; each email examined once                                                              |
| Action items extracted into tasks, linked to the source                                                             | Asks from urgent and important mail become to-dos linked to the email; Scribe meeting action items on request from the note                                                                                        |
| Gmail or Outlook                                                                                                    | Outlook (Microsoft 365), as used by the firm                                                                                                                                                                       |

## Brief and alerts

| alfred_                                                               | Aeka Workspace                                                                                                                                  |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Daily Brief: decisions, drafts, tasks due, calendar, noticed patterns | Calendar with day health, clashes and free time; mail needing you; waiting and chasing; critical and due to-dos; deadlines; reminders; Patterns |
| Stale (7 days) and critical (3 days overdue) tasks escalated          | Same thresholds; critical to-dos lead the brief and alert once                                                                                  |
| 7 am text; SMS nudges                                                 | Notifications in the workspace and on WhatsApp (brief, reminders, urgent mail, meetings)                                                        |

## Tasks

| alfred_                                                   | Aeka Workspace                                                                                                             |
| --------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Natural language input with date, #list and ! priority    | Same, in India time, read without AI, with a one-line "read as" and undo; section and form numbers are never read as times |
| Recurring tasks (daily, weekly, every two weeks, monthly) | Same; the next one is created on completion                                                                                |
| Source badges                                             | Badges open the email, sent email or Scribe note                                                                           |
| Kanban boards                                             | To do, Doing, Done board, with buttons and drag                                                                            |
| (none)                                                    | To-dos move onto a mandate as Workflow tasks; reminders with recurrence; chat                                              |

## Calendar

| alfred_                                               | Aeka Workspace                                                                                                                                            |
| ----------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Meeting load, conflicts, focus time, day rating       | Seven-day strip with a health score (formula shown on screen), meeting and focus hours, clashes, free time                                                |
| Meeting briefs: who, what, what to prepare            | Prep pack without AI: people with last email and follow-up state, mandate tasks and deadlines, recent mail, notes, to-dos; AI briefing on request, cached |
| Conflicts on incoming meeting requests; suggest times | Invitations checked against the calendar; free time found in chat                                                                                         |
| RSVP, create events, focus-time blocks                | Not done: calendar access is read only by choice. Available as a next step with `Calendars.ReadWrite`                                                     |

## Beyond alfred_

Client mandates throughout (mail, meetings and prep linked to Workflow mandates); firm-wide
permissions and ethical walls; every AI call metered with a daily limit and a firm switch;
confidential senders kept from AI; India time and Indian tax context; meeting notes from Scribe;
everything works from a phone.
